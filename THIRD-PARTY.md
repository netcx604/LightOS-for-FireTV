# Third-party components

- **GhostLock** – one-shot root tool for the Fire TV Stick 2 (tank), included
  under `ghostlock/` with its own `LICENSE`. See
  https://github.com/Anezium/ghostlock-firetv-tank and the port for build
  688806320 at https://github.com/mhabou/ghostlock-firetv-tank-688806320.
- **FTVLaunchX** – optional third-party accessibility helper; **not included**.
  Use the upstream project and respect its license.
- **Amazon Fire OS firmware** – not included; kept privately for archival and
  recovery research. Do not redistribute it.
- **Amazon system APK** `com.amazon.vizzini.apk` – not included; Amazon
  proprietary. Provide your own copy only for personal restore.

The LightOS app, scripts and documentation are the project author's own work.
No license has been added for them yet; choose one before public distribution.
